Pricing & Inventory

Prices, sale prices, and stock counts live on each product row. There are no separate price books or warehouses in the free plugin, so keep these fields accurate.

Field behavior

Field Stored as Front-end usage
Price products.price (float) Primary number rendered on cards, detail page, cart, and checkout.
Sale Price products.sale_price (float or null) If set and lower than price, templates show the strikethrough compare price.
Stock products.stock (integer) Displayed as “IN STOCK (x AVAILABLE)” on cards and detail page. Cart/checkout prevent adding more than the stock value.
Status products.status Draft products never appear on storefront, regardless of stock.

Update workflow

  1. Open any product from Kitbix Commerce → Products.
  2. Adjust Price and (optionally) Sale Price. The UI stores raw numbers; currency symbols are applied at render time via kitbix_commerce_format_currency().
  3. Set Stock to the on-hand quantity. There are no separate “track inventory” toggles.
  4. Click Update Product. ProductController::update() saves the numbers and updates the updated_at timestamp.
  5. Reload the storefront grid to confirm price/sale badges and stock counts refresh instantly.

Automation

  • REST API: PATCH /kitbix-commerce/v1/admin/products/{id} with {"price": 69, "stock": 48} to sync from ERPs.
  • Hooks: Subscribe to kitbix_commerce_product_saved to trigger webhooks after price changes.
  • Storefront math: Cart and checkout totals always recalc using the saved product price, not cached values.

Common mistakes

  • Setting sale price higher than price—the UI allows it, but storefront cards will still show the lower number as “Sale”.
  • Leaving stock at “0” after restocks, which makes every product show “In stock (0 available)”.
  • Editing the database directly; you’ll skip sanitization and the kitbix_commerce_product_saved hook.
Reminder: Always update prices/stock via the admin SPA or REST endpoints so Category syncing, meta hooks, and timestamps remain accurate.
