Order Management

Fulfill, refund, and communicate with customers from the Kitbix admin SPA.

What you will cover

Orders live inside Kitbix tables. The React admin provides filters, bulk actions, and timeline history to keep fulfillment predictable.

Step-by-step

  1. Open Kitbix Commerce → Orders to monitor incoming purchases.
  2. Filter by status, payment method, or date to triage tasks.
  3. Open an order to view timeline notes, download permissions, and resend emails.
  4. Update statuses (processing, completed, refunded) as fulfillment progresses.
  5. Export orders via CSV or REST when syncing with ERPs or shipping tools.

Tips & best practices

Tip: Leave timeline notes whenever manual adjustments occur.
Tip: Use bulk status updates only after filtering to avoid accidental mass changes.

Common mistakes

  • Editing totals manually instead of issuing gateway refunds.
  • Neglecting to resend download permissions after replacing files.
Reminder: Always verify payment status at the gateway before marking orders completed.
